== Reading
- Roster talent is what the early route lacks. Against outcomes it improves
  games 1-9 by -0.0105 ± 0.0087 log loss (Brier agrees, resolved), in 8 of
  9 seasons, with a stable coefficient (0.072-0.078 from 2018-19 on; 0.056
  in the games-10+ base logit). The market's correction of the early model
  follows talent_diff (corr +0.42 ± 0.12): roster talent is much of what the
  market knows in October and the carryover model does not.
- Leakage tripwire (G7: a gain above 0.008 a season): the gain is above it,
  so it was checked. talent_fn reads the box score strictly before the date,
  minutes roles from games before it and last season's BPM; the logits and
  composite weights are walk-forward. The arm does not beat the close (it
  narrows the gap: -0.0124 / +0.0068 vs -0.0080 / +0.0125), which leakage
  would. The size fits what is added: the early route had no player
  information at all, where the games-10+ routes already carry the rating
  of this season's games.
- Against the close, per season, unresolved (n 138 each): early_tal better
  than early in both priced seasons (-0.0045, -0.0057).
- H1: its hindsight profit sat in the bets roster talent was against (n 77,
  +32.4% ± 33.2) rather than those it backed (n 38, -11.8% ± 33.3);
  unresolved, descriptive. Shipping early_tal would change H1's and H6's
  bets (both are defined on the early model's P), so the owner decides that
  together with the model change.
- Next (G7): walk-forward with production routing on the priced seasons
  (done here for games 1-9: not worse on either), then refit, rescore, a new
  MODEL_TAG, and the History backtest (G10).
