== Reading
- Updating BPM in season does not help. The Bayes swap is worse on 2024-25
  (+0.0049 ± 0.0038, Brier agrees) and ties on 2025-26; it fails G7.
- Given both, the fit keeps the weight on last season's value (0.054-0.055 vs
  0.013-0.021 in season) and gains nothing (+0.0011 / -0.0004).
- The in-season value overlaps the team rating: in v6_bpmb delta's weight
  falls from 0.017 to 0.011-0.012 (BPM to date carries the team's net rating
  and this season's box production, which the four-factor rating already
  reads). What talent_diff adds is the pre-season view of the roster, which
  this season's results do not improve on.
- Same reading as in-season on/off and Win Shares (CLAUDE.md, guidelines §4).

Caveats: the base route only (production's games 10+ mostly use the
availability logit, whose av_bpm reads the same frozen values; not retested);
M0 = 750 fixed in advance (the free-weight arm does not depend on it);
reconstructed hindsight seasons as in the other Team quality arms.
